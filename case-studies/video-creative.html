Video Creative: Paid, Organic, and the Scripts Behind Them

A mix of paid and organic work — ads that borrow an organic feel, organic pieces that later got put behind spend, and everything in between. Most of this I shot and edited myself; a few I directed and handed to someone else to cut. All of it started as a script.

The range

Paid ads and organic content that don’t always stay in their lane: comedy skits, a product explainer, a fundraiser update, a two-part brand story, a customer testimonial, seasonal promos. Some of it was built for spend from day one. Some of it started as a regular post and earned a budget after it worked.

The process

Every piece starts as a shot list — timing, visual, audio, on-screen text, mapped out before anyone picks up a camera. Below are 3 full scripts, exactly as handed off for filming, including the two-part “Bros on a Mission” sequence that’s in the reel above.

The hands-on part

Most of what’s here I shot and cut myself. A few I directed and someone else edited. I’m not precious about which — the deliverable is what matters, and I can do either end of it.

Script 1 — “Shofar Is Calling”

ROSH HASHANAH CATERING PREORDER · ~15–20s · SOUND ON

Concept: “can hear it” — built to be heard mid-scroll, not just seen.

TimeVisualAudioOn-screen text
0–2sExtreme close-up of the shofar. Instrument only, no context yet.A terrible, off-key attempt at the shofar. Ugly, dry sound.—
2–4sZoom out reveals Sam blowing the shofar (clearly badly), leaning out the window. It lands that it’s him and he’s struggling.Same painful shofar sound.(optional, small) “Sam, warming up.”
6–8sComedic cutaway: birds scattering + a deer startles, snaps its head up, stares. Sells that the sound was absurdly loud → funny.Wings flapping / nature reacting. Comedy beat.—
8–14sCut back to Sam, now improvising to camera, relaxed and charming, talking Rosh Hashanah and inviting people to preorder catering.Festive music kicks in as Sam starts talking. VO = Sam improvising.—
14–18sEnd card / concept card: pun line + preorder CTA with deadline.Festive music resolves and ends here.“Shofar Is Calling. Can you hear it?” → Preorder Rosh Hashanah catering — order by [deadline]

Talking points for the on-camera improv (shot 4): Rosh Hashanah’s coming — this year the only horn you’ll hear is the shofar, not you fighting over who cooks. We’ve got the whole spread — brisket, kugel, challah, the works — ready to preorder. Order early, skip the kitchen, actually enjoy the holiday.

Script 2 — “Knee Injury”

TREND HOOK → BAGEL PUNCHLINE · ~15–20s · SOUND ON

Concept: ride a trend hook (Coco delivery robots), land the punchline on the actual product.

TimeVisualAudioOn-screen text
0–3sSam walking down the sidewalk. In frame, a Coco delivery robot rolling down the street — the new thing in the city. Hold a beat so it reads as the hook.Street ambient. Sam humming / casual.—
3–6sSam “bumps” into the Coco — exaggerated and intentionally fake. Clearly forced, not a real collision. Lean into the bit.Cartoonish bump SFX.—
6–9sSam drops to the ground, groaning that his knees hurt.Sam: “Ah, my knees…”—
9–11sStill on the ground, he says the only thing that’d make him feel better is a bagel. He looks up — the Zeitlin’s sign is right there.Sam: “Only thing that’d fix this is a bagel.” Beat. “…huh, I think this place has bagels.”—
11–13sSilent-movie transition: black card, old-film title style.Music sting / needle-drop. Beat of silence.“A few moments later”
13–17sSam inside Zeitlin’s, happily eating a bagel.Sam, content: “My knees feel so much better.”(optional) Logo + “Zeitlin’s”
17–18sFade to black.Music out.(optional CTA) —

Script 3 — “Bros on a Mission” (2-part)

RENOVATION ANNOUNCEMENT · SOUND ON · BOTH PARTS IN THE REEL ABOVE

Purpose: announce that construction was starting at the restaurant. Concept: wordless teaser builds anticipation, paid off in Part B with the permit announcement and demo day.

Part A — Teaser (“…Continue”)
TimeVisualAudioOn-screen text
0–3sThe Zeitlin’s bros shot from behind, walking toward / into a Home Depot.Confident, driving music bed. No dialogue.“The Zeitlin’s bros are on a mission.”
3–8sCut to them entering the store. They walk serious and focused — dead-ahead stares, moving down the aisles like they’re on a mission, until they find the aisle they want.Music continues. Footsteps. No dialogue.—
8–10sDetail insert: a hand grabs a hammer off the shelf.Beat / SFX on the grab.—
10–13sCut to gloves (or another construction item). Two hands grab it and they walk out — in confident, out confident, taking exactly what’s needed.Music.—
13–15sEnd card.Music cuts on the card.“…Continue” (to-be-continued style)

Intent: no explicit hook or VO — just “they’re on a mission.” Pure curiosity so people follow the story into Part B.

Part B — Payoff (permits + demo day)
TimeVisualAudioOn-screen text
0–3sThe Zeitlin’s bros inside the store, announcing the news to camera.VO/dialogue: the city granted the permits to start the renovation.(optional) “It’s official.”
3–8sThey pick up the hammers and gear from Part A and start breaking things — demo begins.Big impact SFX, energetic music.—
8–12sQuick montage of the first demo hits.Music peaks.(optional) “Renovation starts now.”
12–15sEnd card.Music resolves.“Zeitlin’s is getting a makeover — [reopen date / follow for updates]”
